# Isleta 1.1.2

Notifications no longer disappear when you click somewhere else.

## Fixed

- **A notification stays long enough to read.** Clicking elsewhere still closes an island *you*
  opened, but no longer one that opened itself — the click that carried on whatever you were doing
  was taking the message away first. Escape, clicking the island, or swiping it up still close it.

## Requirements

macOS 26 or later, on Apple Silicon. Displays with a real notch get the island; a Mac with none gets
a synthesized island on the primary display.

## Known limitations

- **Brightness has no HUD.** macOS offers no public way to read display brightness on Apple Silicon.
- **Apple's own HUDs are not replaced.** Isleta shows its own alongside them.
- **The island sits above Mission Control.** In a real notch it covers nothing; a synthesized island
  can cover the centre of the space labels.
- **A burst of notifications opens the island once for each.** Grouping them is a later release's
  job.
- **Playing music costs more than it should.** About 7.7% CPU against an internal 4% budget for an
  animating island, and a 250–290 MB memory footprint against a 60 MB one, both reproduced across
  four runs. It is not a leak: resident memory is flat and the footprint plateaus rather than
  climbing. Idle is unaffected — around 0.04% with nothing on stage, well inside its own 0.3%
  budget. Earlier releases quoted 0.4% here; that figure is superseded, and how it was arrived at
  could not be established.
