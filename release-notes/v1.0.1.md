# Isleta 1.0.1

A maintenance release. The island now gets out of the way when you move between desktops and
fullscreen apps, and the Welcome Back moment opens by itself.

## Fixed

**Launch at login works.** In 1.0.0 the switch was greyed out with "this copy of Isleta is not code
signed" under it, on a copy that was signed and notarized. macOS reports a login item it has never
seen before and one it cannot register in ways that are easy to confuse, and Isleta believed the
wrong one — so the switch was dead for anyone who had not already turned it on, which is everyone.
It is live now, and if the system does refuse, it says why instead of deciding in advance.

**The island no longer travels with a switching workspace.** Moving between spaces left it painted
into the slide — pinned over two desktops sailing past, or welded to the arriving fullscreen app and
drifting away from the notch, before snapping back to where it had been all along. It now goes off
screen the instant a switch begins and arrives back at the notch with its usual bounce.

The cause is that a space transition photographs the screen rather than compositing it live, so the
island had to be gone before the picture was taken — a few milliseconds after the switch starts, and
well before anything can be redrawn. Every transition behaves differently: a desktop slide announces
itself one way, entering a fullscreen app another, and leaving one a third, with nothing at all
marking the end. Isleta now hides on anything that might be a switch and comes back once the system
stops reporting, which is the only account of "finished" that holds for all of them.

**Settings opens in front, and in the middle.** The window used to arrive behind whatever app you
were using — drawn correctly, but without focus, so the switches worked while the window looked
dead and Escape went somewhere else. On a Mac that had never opened it, it also came up flush
against the left edge and low instead of centred. Both are fixed, and a window you have moved
yourself still opens where you left it.

**Quitting no longer strands a helper process.** Isleta reads Now Playing through a small `perl`
helper, and an ordinary Quit was ending the app while the helper was still being told to stop —
one orphan per launch, quietly accumulating until a restart. Teardown now waits for it, and any
strays left behind by an earlier crash are cleared at the next launch.

**The status menu no longer shows a ⌘, next to "Open Isleta Settings".** Isleta has no menu bar to
dispatch it from, so the shortcut it advertised opened the settings of whichever app was frontmost.
Clicking the item does what it always did.

## Improved

**Welcome Back opens on its own.** The greeting used to wait behind a click. It now presents itself
when you come back to the Mac, with a second line under the greeting drawn from a set written for
the hour — morning, afternoon, evening and night each have their own, and the night ones know what
time it is.

**The app icon's ripples are lighter**, which keeps them from over-brightening in dark mode.

## Requirements

Unchanged from 1.0.0: macOS 26 or later, Apple Silicon, and a display with a real notch — or a
synthesized island on the primary display for a Mac that has none.

## Notes and limitations

Everything listed under 1.0.0 still applies. One addition worth knowing:

- **The island takes a moment to return after a space switch** — about a third of a second once the
  system has confirmed the switch finished. macOS provides no signal for the end of a space
  transition, so Isleta waits for the reports to stop rather than guessing; coming back sooner means
  reappearing in the middle of the animation.
